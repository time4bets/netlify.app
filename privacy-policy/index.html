Privacy Policy

This policy explains how an informational website may handle basic technical and contact-related data.

Updated: May 2026

Scope of this policy

This page applies to the Time4Bets informational website. It explains the type of information that may be processed when visitors browse pages, use links or contact the site owner.

Technical information

Standard technical data may be collected by hosting, analytics or security tools. This can include browser type, approximate location, device information, page requests and time of visit.

  • IP-related logs may be stored by hosting providers.
  • Analytics data should be used to understand site performance.
  • Security logs may help prevent spam or abuse.

Personal information

The site does not need sensitive personal information for normal browsing. If a visitor sends a message, the submitted details may be used only to answer that request.

Third-party links

Some buttons or links may lead to external websites. Those websites operate under their own privacy rules, so visitors should review the policies on the destination site.

Data control

Visitors can limit cookies through browser settings and avoid sending personal information through forms when they do not want to be contacted.
