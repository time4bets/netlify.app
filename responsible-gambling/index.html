Responsible Gambling

Use gambling content only as entertainment, with clear limits and full control over time and money.

Updated: May 2026

Entertainment only

Gambling should never be treated as a way to earn money or solve financial problems. It involves risk, and losses can happen quickly.

Set limits first

Before using any gambling service, decide how much time and money you can afford to spend. Do not increase limits because of emotion, pressure or previous losses.

  • Use a fixed budget.
  • Set a session time limit.
  • Take breaks before making new deposits.
  • Do not chase losses.

Warning signs

Stop and seek help if gambling starts to affect sleep, work, relationships, rent, bills or emotional stability. Hiding activity or borrowing money to gamble are serious warning signs.

Self-exclusion and support

Many platforms and regions offer account limits, cooling-off periods or self-exclusion tools. Use them early if control becomes difficult.

Adults only

This website is not intended for minors. Keep gambling accounts, payment details and promotional content away from children and underage users.
